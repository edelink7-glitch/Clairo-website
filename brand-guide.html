Color Usage

Ink is the page. Use it as the default background for all primary surfaces.

Charcoal lifts secondary surfaces off the base — ticker bars, card fills, and dividers.

Bone is the single accent. Use sparingly — hero highlights, CTA borders, hover underlines.
