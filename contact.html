Common Questions

What types of projects do you take on?

I work across commercial video, brand content, short form, real estate media, and event coverage. If it requires premium production, I'm interested. I don't do commodity work — every project I take on gets my full creative attention.

How far in advance should I book?

For individual shoots, 1–2 weeks minimum is ideal. For larger campaigns or multi-day productions, 3–4 weeks gives us the time to do the project proper justice. For real estate, we often accommodate same-week requests when availability permits.

Do you work outside Montreal?

Yes — we're available for projects across Quebec and Canada. We regularly work in the surrounding region and can travel for the right project. If you're outside Montreal, reach out and we'll work out the details.

What's your pricing like?

Every project is scoped individually based on scope, deliverables, and timeline. I don't post rates because no two projects are the same. Book a call and I'll give you a clear picture within 24 hours of speaking. No surprises, no padding.

What does the process look like?

I start with a strategy call to understand your goals, audience, and competitive landscape. From there, I build a production plan, shoot on a confirmed date, handle all post-production, and deliver final assets optimized for your channels. You're involved at every key decision point.

How long does delivery take?

Real estate photography is typically delivered within 48–72 hours. Commercial projects and video productions range from 5–14 business days depending on scope. Rush delivery is available for time-sensitive work — just ask upfront.
